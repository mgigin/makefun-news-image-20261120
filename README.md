One image that sums up the world's news of **Friday 20 November 2026**: 35
date-checked stories across 7 regions — a stat band, a UTC timeline, three
illustrated lead stories (Russian missiles hit Kyiv's bridges, Cyclone Akvan
landfall in India, an AI sell-off sinking Asia's markets), ten more story
cards, a world map with numbered pins, 22 further stories, and a date-check
box listing the previous days' news left out on purpose.

Built for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120).
`main.ts` copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`; `build_image.py` is the
deterministic generator that drew the poster (original drawings, no network).
